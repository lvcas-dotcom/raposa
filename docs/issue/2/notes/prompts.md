# Prompts da identidade visual

Ferramenta: geração integrada de imagens (`image_gen`), sem CLI ou uso de chave de API.
Data: 2026-10-21. A proposta de mascote foi aprovada pelo mantenedor na conversa.
Arquivos: `img/icon-open-rabbit-v2.png` e `img/banner-open-rabbit-v2.png`.
As referências anteriores foram preservadas. A origem generativa não equivale a
uma declaração de exclusividade de marca ou a uma busca jurídica de anterioridade.

## Ícone

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: square app/repository icon for an independent open-source code-review project, Open Rabbit.
Input image role: the supplied icon is a COLOR AND SIMPLICITY REFERENCE ONLY. Replace the entire rabbit design. Preserve only the general warm orange background / white mark direction.
Primary request: design a polished ORIGINAL rabbit superhero mascot emblem, visually independent and ownable as a design direction. This must look like a new professional software brand, not an altered version of the reference mascot.
Subject: an upright FRONT-FACING rabbit with an expressive face, one tall straight ear and one clearly bent ear, a broad compact head with squared rounded cheeks, two orange negative-space eyes, and a small diamond nose. A short angular cape frames the head and shoulders and folds into a broad V below the chin. Use bold, simple interlocking white shapes and orange negative space. Friendly, alert, clever and confident, not childish or aggressive.
Composition: one compact integrated emblem centered on a square solid warm vermilion-orange background (#F46025). Large recognizable silhouette occupying about 76% of the image, generous equal outer clear space. Face and cape should remain legible at 40px.
Style: crisp flat vector-like identity artwork, limited to white and the single orange background. Beautiful intentional curves, strong proportion and balanced negative space. No outlines or intricate detail.
Text: none.
Constraints: fully redesign pose, silhouette, face, ears, body proportions and cape. Do NOT draw a seated rabbit in right-facing profile; do NOT trace or preserve the silhouette of the input reference. No keyhole, no circular neck medallion, no borrowed logo, no watermark, no mockup, no presentation board, no 3D, no shadows, no gradients. A single finished square icon only.
```

## Banner

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: a FINISHED WIDE GitHub README banner for Open Rabbit, landscape aspect ratio EXACTLY 3:1, approximately 2400 by 800 pixels. Not a square, not a mockup.
Input image 1 role: MASCOT IDENTITY MASTER. Reuse the exact new original front-facing rabbit face and V-shaped superhero cape from image 1: tall ear on viewer's left, bent ear on viewer's right, cheek shapes, eyes, nose, smile and cape geometry. Preserve its identity and proportions, don't invent another rabbit.
Input image 2 role: LAYOUT REFERENCE ONLY for a wide orange banner with a white symbol and project name. Completely replace the old side-profile rabbit; do not copy its mark or typography.
Primary request: create a beautiful restrained horizontal brand lockup. On the left, the new rabbit emblem from image 1, modestly sized with all ears and cape comfortably inside the canvas. To its right, a single line reading exactly "Open Rabbit" in a clean modern distinctive geometric sans-serif wordmark, medium-bold weight, excellent kerning, clean letterforms. This is an independent project's own wordmark. Keep a real visual space between the two words.
Scene/backdrop: warm orange background matching input image 1, uninterrupted edge to edge. Foreground entirely white. No frame.
Composition: balanced large clear space at both ends and vertically. Emblem and wordmark together occupy around 76 percent of banner width and 64 percent of banner height. Symbol approximately 24 percent width, name approximately 48 percent, graceful gap. Align wordmark optical center with rabbit face/cape mass, not ear tips. Make the wordmark easy to read when displayed 640 pixels wide.
Style: polished flat vector-like software brand identity, elegant and intentional, no stock logo presentation effects.
Text (verbatim): "Open Rabbit". No other text.
Constraints: same exact original mascot as input 1. No old side-profile rabbit from image 2. No extra slogan, no watermark, no icons, no objects, no drop shadows, no 3D, no decorative code, no cropping. Deliver only the finished 3:1 banner.
```
